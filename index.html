Introduction to Lean-Nexus · 2:36

AI-based mathematical formalization and verification tools. Lean-Nexus formalizes research mathematics into machine-checked, Lean-based proofs.

01

Verifies every reasoning step

Each step of an argument is checked by the Lean proof assistant, not just read.

02

Re-derives every computation

Calculations in the paper are recomputed rather than taken on trust.

03

Localizes the exact line where an argument breaks

When something fails, you see precisely where, so authors and referees can act on it.
